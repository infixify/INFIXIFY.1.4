🗂️ Formula Keys & Board Derivations

Properties of Adjoint (Matrices)
|adj(A)| = |A|^(n-1)
adj(adj(A)) = |A|^(n-2) · A
adj(AB) = adj(B) · adj(A)
Current Electricity — Drift Velocity
vd = eEτ / m
Temperature Dependence of Resistance
R = R₀(1 + αΔT)
Raoult's Law — Deviations
Positive deviation: A–B interactions weaker than A–A/B–B, Ptotal > ideal.
Negative deviation: A–B interactions stronger, Ptotal < ideal.
Van 't Hoff Factor
i = 1 + (n − 1)α — for dissociation into n particles with degree α.
Derive Lens Maker's Formula
Apply refraction at two spherical surfaces of the lens, add the results using sign convention to get 1/f = (n−1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂).
Electric Field — Infinite Line Charge (Gauss's Law)
Use a cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius r, length L. Flux = E·(2πrL) = λL/ε₀ ⟹ E = λ/(2πε₀r).
Boundary Conditions of Wave Interference
Path difference for constructive: Δ = nλ; destructive: Δ = (n+½)λ. Derive from superposition of two coherent waves with phase difference φ.
